A Letter to the Residents of Ward 7

Dear Residents,

Thank you for taking the time to read this Community Blueprint.

Ward 7 is a remarkable place to call home. It is where families are building their future, seniors have created lasting memories, entrepreneurs have invested their dreams, and neighbours from every background come together to create one of the most diverse and welcoming communities in Mississauga.

Like many of you, I care deeply about the future of our neighbourhoods. We all want safe streets, reliable City services, responsible use of taxpayer dollars, thriving local businesses, well-maintained parks, and opportunities for our children and future generations.

These goals are not achieved through promises alone. They require thoughtful planning, careful listening, collaboration, and leadership that focuses on practical solutions rather than politics.

That is why I am running to serve as your Ward 7 Councillor.

Throughout my career, I have had the opportunity to work in engineering, public transit, retail management, business ownership, immigration consulting, and real estate. Each experience has given me a different perspective on how people live, work, and overcome challenges. More importantly, they have taught me that every decision affects real people, real families, and real businesses.

As a husband and father, I understand that the decisions made at City Hall shape everyday life. They influence how safely our children walk to school, how long we spend in traffic, the condition of our parks and sidewalks, the strength of our local economy, and the quality of the services we rely on every day.

This Community Blueprint is not simply an election platform. It is a practical plan that reflects the values I will bring to public office and the priorities I will advance when I am elected as your Councillor.

It has been developed with one principle in mind:

Residents First

The best decisions begin with listening. Throughout this campaign, I will continue meeting residents, business owners, community organizations, and neighbourhood groups because the strongest ideas often come from the people who experience these challenges every day.

No Councillor can solve every issue alone. Progress requires respectful collaboration with residents, City staff, fellow members of Council, and our provincial and federal partners. My commitment is to approach every issue with an open mind, honest communication, and a determination to find practical solutions.

Ward 7 is entering an important period of change. New development, growing infrastructure needs, and changing community expectations present both challenges and opportunities. We have the chance to build a community that is safer, more connected, financially responsible, and prepared for the future while preserving the welcoming character that makes Ward 7 such a special place to live.

I believe we can achieve that future together.

Thank you for your trust. I look forward to listening, learning, and working with you to build a stronger Ward 7 for everyone.

Sincerely,

Amitabh Srivastava, B.Eng.
Candidate for Mississauga Ward 7 Councillor

About Me

Amitabh Srivastava is a husband, father, engineer, and community-minded entrepreneur running for Ward 7 Councillor. Raised with the values of service and responsibility, he has built a career helping families, workers, and newcomers navigate complex systems with clarity and respect. From engineering and public transit to small business ownership, immigration consulting, and real estate, Amitabh has focused on practical problem-solving and listening to people. He understands the challenges Ward 7 families face because he lives them too: balancing work, community, and the everyday needs of a growing neighbourhood. Amitabh is committed to transparent leadership, responsible spending, and a City Hall that puts residents first.

The Ward 7 Community Blueprint

A Practical Plan for a Safer, Stronger Ward 7

Residents First

Putting residents first means more than listening during an election campaign. It means making residents the starting point of every decision at City Hall.

When I am elected as your Ward 7 Councillor, I will remain accessible, listen before making decisions, explain my position clearly and follow residents' concerns from the first report to the final response.

My background in engineering, transit, business, and housing informs a practical approach to City Hall.

These experiences have prepared me to understand issues from different perspectives and to bring a practical, organized and accountable approach to City Hall.

Residents First will happen through five clear priorities:

  • Safe Streets—Strong Communities
  • Responsible Spending—Responsive Services
  • Prepare Today—Protect Tomorrow
  • Build Smart—Grow Responsibly
  • Local Business—Shared Prosperity
Priority 1

Safe Streets. Strong Communities.

Ward 7 residents can move through their neighbourhoods safely, confidently, and without preventable road hazards.

  • Safer intersections, school zones, and pedestrian crossings
  • Faster action on speeding, visibility, and lighting concerns
  • Better access to transit, parks, and community routes
See details

As a parent and community member, I understand the concern of families who want safer routes to school, transit, and local amenities. My engineering background helps me evaluate traffic patterns and infrastructure needs carefully so decisions are based on evidence, not guesswork.

Priority 2

Responsible Spending. Responsive Services.

Residents get clear, timely service and more value from every tax dollar spent.

  • Stronger 311 follow-up and repair tracking
  • Clearer service timelines and public reporting
  • Better contractor accountability and lower waste
See details

Residents deserve city services that are easy to access, honest about delays, and accountable for results. My business background has taught me that disciplined budgeting and clear follow-up are essential for keeping services responsive and cost-effective.

Priority 3

Prepare Today. Protect Tomorrow.

Ward 7 stays ahead of flooding, maintenance issues, and climate risks before they become emergencies.

  • Priority fixes for flooding and drainage hotspots
  • Better stormwater planning and road maintenance
  • Clear emergency communication and resident guidance
See details

Known risks should be documented, prioritized, and addressed before storm damage escalates. My engineering background informs a practical, prevention-first approach to infrastructure so residents are not left dealing with repeated repairs and disruption.

Priority 4

Build Smart. Grow Responsibly.

Growth strengthens neighbourhoods without overwhelming roads, transit, parks, or housing affordability.

  • Housing that matches family, rental, and senior needs
  • Infrastructure and transit planned with new development
  • Protection of parks, neighbourhood character, and public space
See details

Ward 7 will continue to grow, but development must improve residents' quality of life. I will push for housing choices that are affordable, well-connected, and supported by roads, transit, and community services that already exist or are planned.

Priority 5

Local Business. Shared Prosperity.

Local businesses thrive, seniors stay connected, and young people see a future in Ward 7.

  • Reliable access and wayfinding during construction
  • Support for entrepreneurs, youth, and community programming
  • Stronger neighbourhood identity through local business growth
See details

Strong communities depend on the people and businesses that make them vibrant. As a former business owner, I understand how construction, municipal delays, and poor access can damage local trade. I want Ward 7 to support small business opportunity, youth engagement, and active community spaces.

Accountability

A platform is only meaningful when residents can see how it is put into practice and how progress is measured.

Accessible office

Residents and businesses will have a direct, responsive way to raise concerns, ask questions, and get updates.

Clear updates

I will share regular progress reports so Ward 7 residents know what is moving, what is delayed, and why.

Transparent issue tracking

Major concerns will be tracked consistently, followed up on, and reviewed with residents as work progresses.

Annual progress report

Each year, I will publish a clear summary of completed work, ongoing priorities, and next steps for the ward.

First 100 Days

  • Establish an organized Ward 7 office
  • Begin a community listening tour
  • Review priority intersections, drainage, and infrastructure concerns
  • Meet local businesses, residents, and community groups
  • Examine major development and capital files
  • Create a transparent system for tracking resident issues
