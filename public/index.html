A little about me

Real estate is about people, not just property.

I'm Amitabh—a husband, father, entrepreneur, small business owner, and proud first-generation Canadian. More than two decades ago, I came to Canada looking for opportunity and a future for the family I hoped to build.

My path has taken me through electrical engineering, immigration consulting, business ownership, and real estate. Those experiences taught me to listen closely, work through complexity, and help people make important decisions with care.

Today, I bring that perspective to every real estate conversation. My goal is simple: understand what matters to you, explain your options clearly, and help you take the next step on your terms.
